The problem

Service accounts, API keys, deploy keys, and OAuth apps now outnumber human identities in most cloud environments.

They are rarely reviewed, frequently over-scoped, and almost never mapped to what they can actually reach. When one leaks, teams can see that it leaked — not what it can touch, or what happens if it's used right now.

How it works

Evidence → Decision → Remediation → Verify

01 · DISCOVER

Reachability graph

Builds a real graph of what each identity can reach from connected identity and asset data.

02 · DECIDE

Deterministic rules

A rule engine turns the graph into ranked findings. No model in the decision loop.

03 · REMEDIATE

Gated action

Runs only after dual human approval and a live kill-switch check, re-verified on every call.

04 · VERIFY

Real outcome

Re-checks the live environment and records the true result, not the intended one.

Every step is written to a hash-chained, append-only audit log that even the application cannot edit or delete — enforced at the database level.
